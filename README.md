# Intessika

> **Blazing-fast local-first context manager** — capture, keep, create, annotate, and more in private. Your data stays on your device. Always.

## 💻 Supported Platforms

Intessika will be available for desktop computers running:
* **Windows** (10 and 11)
* **macOS**
* **Mobile (Planned):** Companion apps for iOS and Android are on our roadmap.

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Local-First:** Processing and storage stay on your machine — your data never leaves your device.
* **Offline by Design:** No account, no cloud, no internet required for core use. AI is never forced.
* **Context Hub:** Capture, triage, and annotate — keep all your context in one place.
* **Fast Search:** Find any piece of context instantly.
* **Files on Disk:** Real OS files — annotate and organize without a proprietary vault. Sync across devices with Drive, Dropbox, Syncthing, or any tool you choose.
* **Cognitive Relief:** Advanced tree colormapping, visual salience, and document analytics designed to reduce mental fatigue.

## 🧠 Cognitive Relief & UI Philosophy

Intessika is built to reduce friction when managing complex knowledge — not another monochrome file list.

* **Visual Saliency:** Tree colormapping, badges, and tags create an "information scent" so critical context stands out before you read every line.
* **Dense, Purposeful UI:** High information density, low clutter — structure and color carry meaning, not decoration.
* **Hub-and-Spoke:** Move between documents, annotations, and sketches and the macro-tree without losing your place.
* **Document Analytics:** Usage signals help resurface what matters and reveal connections you would otherwise miss.
* **Built for Speed:** Rust-powered performance keeps the tree responsive as the workspace grows.

## 🔒 Privacy & License

* **No Vendor Lock-In:** Intessika reads files directly from your OS filesystem. Your documents remain standard files that you can open, move, or back up with any third-party tool. You can migrate at any time and keep everything.
* **Sync Your Way:** Because files live on disk, sync across devices with any tool you already use — Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Syncthing, and more. Intessika does not lock you into its own cloud.
* **Your Files Stay Yours:** Work with external Office documents, draw, and annotate. Organize everything in your tree structure — Intessika never alters your original files.
* **Source Code:** The Intessika Core app is proprietary.
* **Extensibility (Planned):** We plan to support custom widgets and extensions with an open contribution path, allowing the community to extend functionality without exposing the core code.
